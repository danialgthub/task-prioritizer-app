You add your tasks, each one gets a benefit % and a “Why x%?” breakdown, and the top task is shown as “Start with this”. The score uses five factors:

deadline
impact on your result
skill growth
whether others are waiting on it
hours needed

You can change how much each factor counts under “What matters most”. The app also plans your 9am to 6pm day and shows how many hours are free for certificate study. With only 1 or 2 office tasks, that free block is probably most of your day
